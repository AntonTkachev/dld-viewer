About Trade Center Second

In Trade Center Second over 5 years, 1,039 property transactions have been recorded according to the Dubai Land Department. Median property price — 5.68 M AED. Price per square meter — 50,130 AED. Median area — 138 m².

Trade Center Second is a Dubai district. All transactions are apartments; villas and townhouses are not built here. About 80% are off-plan purchases in under-construction projects. Most common type — 2-bedroom (503 transactions). One of the most active projects — Jumeirah Residences Emirates Towers. 22,672 rental contracts have also been recorded — the district is attractive for both purchase and long-term living.
