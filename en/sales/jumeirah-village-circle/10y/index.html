About Jumeirah Village Circle

In Jumeirah Village Circle over 10 years, 77,499 property transactions have been recorded according to the Dubai Land Department. Median property price — 840,000 AED. Price per square meter — 13,173 AED. Median area — 69 m².

Jumeirah Village Circle is a Dubai district. Mostly apartments — 97% of transactions. About 64% are off-plan purchases in under-construction projects.
