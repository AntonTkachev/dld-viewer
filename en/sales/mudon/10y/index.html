About Mudon

In Mudon over 10 years, 4,411 property transactions have been recorded according to the Dubai Land Department. Median property price — 2.69 M AED. Price per square meter — 12,339 AED. Median area — 189 m².

All-time figures across DLD records.

Mudon is a Dubai district. Mostly villas/townhouses — 93% of transactions. About 52% are off-plan purchases in under-construction projects. Most common type — villas (3,657 transactions).
