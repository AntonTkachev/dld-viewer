About Al Satwa

In Al Satwa over 5 years, 3,098 property transactions have been recorded according to the Dubai Land Department. Median property price — 1.40 M AED. Price per square meter — 23,275 AED. Median area — 64 m².

All-time figures across DLD records.

Al Satwa is a Dubai district. Mostly apartments — 86% of transactions. About 61% are off-plan purchases in under-construction projects. Most common type — 1-bedroom (1,295 transactions). One of the most active projects — Linea By Holm. 52,526 rental contracts have also been recorded — the district is attractive for both purchase and long-term living.
