About Bukadra

In Bukadra over 3 years, 10,524 property transactions have been recorded according to the Dubai Land Department. Median property price — 2.01 M AED. Price per square meter — 26,103 AED. Median area — 78 m².

All-time figures across DLD records.

Bukadra is a Dubai district. All transactions are apartments; villas and townhouses are not built here. About 98% are off-plan purchases in under-construction projects. Most common type — 1-bedroom (6,325 transactions). One of the most active projects — Skyvue. 690 rental contracts have also been recorded — the district is attractive for both purchase and long-term living.
