About Meydan

In Meydan over 10 years, 8,869 property transactions have been recorded according to the Dubai Land Department. Median property price — 2.55 M AED. Price per square meter — 17,235 AED. Median area — 182 m².

Meydan is a Dubai district. Apartments account for 69%, the rest are villas/townhouses. About 43% are off-plan purchases in under-construction projects. Most common type — land plots (2,434 transactions). One of the most active projects — Grand Views. 16,070 rental contracts have also been recorded — the district is attractive for both purchase and long-term living.

Frequently asked questions about Meydan

What is the average price per m² in Meydan?

The median price in Meydan is 15,767 AED per m² (median total price — 2.82 M AED). Based on all registered transactions in the Dubai Land Department.

How many transactions have happened in Meydan?

10,075 sale transactions have been registered in Meydan to date. Source: official DLD register.

Is there a sale transaction history for Meydan?

Yes — DXBCompass lists the full sale transaction history and data for Meydan: 10,075 registered deals with price, date, project and size, sourced directly from the Dubai Land Department and updated weekly.

What is the average rent in Meydan?

The median annual rent in Meydan is 93,500 AED. A total of 16,206 rental contracts (Ejari) are registered.

How much does it cost to rent an apartment or villa in Meydan?

Median annual rent in Meydan by unit type: studio — 53,500 AED; 1-bedroom — 69,000 AED; 2-bedroom — 105,000 AED; 3-bedroom — 127,025 AED; 4+ bedrooms — 240,000 AED; villa — 260,000 AED. Based on registered Ejari contracts in the Dubai Land Department.

Is rental transaction data available for Meydan?

Yes — DXBCompass tracks the rent transaction history for Meydan: 16,206 registered Ejari contracts with rent amount, date and property type, sourced from the Dubai Land Department and updated weekly.

What dominates in Meydan — apartments or villas?

In Meydan, 66% of transactions are apartments, 34% are villas.

What are the most active projects in Meydan?

The leader by transaction volume in Meydan is Grand Views.

Is there a metro station in Meydan?

There is no metro station inside Meydan. The nearest are Business Bay (Red) — 4.8 km from the district, Burj Khalifa / Dubai Mall (Red) — 5.1 km from the district.
