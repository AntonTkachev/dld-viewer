About City Of Arabia

In City Of Arabia over 5 years, 3,808 property transactions have been recorded according to the Dubai Land Department. Median property price — 680,000 AED. Price per square meter — 17,681 AED. Median area — 42 m².

City Of Arabia is a Dubai district. All transactions are apartments; villas and townhouses are not built here. About 97% are off-plan purchases in under-construction projects. Most common type — studios (2,277 transactions). One of the most active projects — Mag 330. 108 rental contracts have also been recorded — the district is attractive for both purchase and long-term living.
