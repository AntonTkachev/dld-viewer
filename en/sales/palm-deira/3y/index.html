About Palm Deira

In Palm Deira over 3 years, 10,261 property transactions have been recorded according to the Dubai Land Department. Median property price — 2.89 M AED. Price per square meter — 26,187 AED. Median area — 115 m².

Palm Deira is a Dubai district. All transactions are apartments; villas and townhouses are not built here. About 89% are off-plan purchases in under-construction projects. Most common type — 1-bedroom (4,239 transactions). One of the most active projects — Bay Villas - Dubai Islands. 845 rental contracts have also been recorded — the district is attractive for both purchase and long-term living.
