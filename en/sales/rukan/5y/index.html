About Rukan

In Rukan over 5 years, 2,603 property transactions have been recorded according to the Dubai Land Department. Median property price — 640,000 AED. Price per square meter — 10,070 AED. Median area — 71 m².

Rukan is a Dubai district. Apartments account for 39%, the rest are villas/townhouses. About 63% are off-plan purchases in under-construction projects. Most common type — villas (1,594 transactions). One of the most active projects — Rukan 3. 2,166 rental contracts have also been recorded — the district is attractive for both purchase and long-term living.
