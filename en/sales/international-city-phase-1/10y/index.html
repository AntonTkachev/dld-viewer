About International City Phase 1

In International City Phase 1 over 10 years, 19,642 property transactions have been recorded according to the Dubai Land Department. Median property price — 365,000 AED. Price per square meter — 6,519 AED. Median area — 61 m².

International City Phase 1 is a Dubai district. Mostly apartments — 94% of transactions. About 15% are off-plan purchases in under-construction projects. Most common type — 1-bedroom (7,215 transactions). One of the most active projects — Lawnz Residence By Danube. 294,125 rental contracts have also been recorded — the district is attractive for both purchase and long-term living.
