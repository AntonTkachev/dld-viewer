About Arjan

In Arjan over 10 years, 23,172 property transactions have been recorded according to the Dubai Land Department. Median property price — 753,503 AED. Price per square meter — 13,246 AED. Median area — 61 m².

Arjan is a Dubai district. All transactions are apartments; villas and townhouses are not built here. About 70% are off-plan purchases in under-construction projects. Most common type — studios (9,692 transactions). One of the most active projects — The Central Downtown. 80,056 rental contracts have also been recorded — the district is attractive for both purchase and long-term living.
