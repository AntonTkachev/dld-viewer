About City Walk

In City Walk over 10 years, 8,875 rental contracts have been registered according to the Dubai Land Department. Median annual rent — 170,000 AED. Rent per area — 1,044 AED/m²/year.

City Walk is a Dubai district. Transaction mix: apartments 89%, land and commercial property 11%. About 72% are off-plan purchases in under-construction projects. One of the most active projects — Verve City Walk. 8,875 rental contracts have also been recorded — the district is attractive for both purchase and long-term living.
