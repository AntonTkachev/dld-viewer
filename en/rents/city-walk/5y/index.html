About City Walk

In City Walk over 5 years, 5,476 rental contracts have been registered according to the Dubai Land Department. Median annual rent — 190,000 AED. Rent per area — 1,262 AED/m²/year.

City Walk is a Dubai district. All transactions are apartments; villas and townhouses are not built here. About 75% are off-plan purchases in under-construction projects.
