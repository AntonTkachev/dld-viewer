About La Mer

In La Mer over 12 months, 749 rental contracts have been registered according to the Dubai Land Department. Median annual rent — 162,000 AED. Rent per area — 1,672 AED/m²/year.

La Mer is a Dubai district. Mostly apartments — 91% of transactions. About 69% are off-plan purchases in under-construction projects. Most common type — 1-bedroom (1,167 transactions). One of the most active projects — Jumeirah Asora Bay. 2,773 rental contracts have also been recorded — the district is attractive for both purchase and long-term living.
